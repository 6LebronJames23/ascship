How it works

Three steps, and none of them involve your Apple ID password.

01 · access

Invite me to your team

Add me to your App Store Connect team as an App Manager and share the project (a private GitHub invite or a zip). It takes two minutes and you can remove me at any time.

02 · ship

I do the Apple part

Signing, build upload, listing, screenshots, App Privacy, age rating, in-app purchases, review notes and the submission itself. I ask you only what I can't know, like what data your app collects.

03 · review

I stay until it's live

If Apple rejects it, I read the message, fix it and resubmit. You get a plain-English note on what happened each time.

Questions

What do you need from me?

An Apple Developer Program membership (Apple charges $99/year), an invite to your App Store Connect team, your project's source, and answers to a few questions about what your app does with user data. If you have screenshots and a description, great. If not, I'll make them from the app.

My app was built with AI tools. Is that a problem?

No. Apps built with Claude, Cursor, Lovable, Replit, Bolt and similar tools are exactly who this is for. Native Swift is home turf. React Native, Expo, Flutter and Capacitor projects are fine as long as the project builds for iOS.

Can you guarantee Apple will approve it?

Nobody honestly can, because the decision is Apple's. What I do promise: rejections caused by the submission itself (signing, metadata, privacy answers, review notes, a missing demo account) are fixed and resubmitted at no extra cost. Some apps break a rule that no fix can get around, and if yours looks like one I'll say so before you pay anything.

How long does it take?

Most apps are submitted within two days of me getting access. After that the clock is Apple's: review usually takes a few days and occasionally longer.

Is my code safe?

I use it only to build and submit your app, and delete my copy when the job is done. Happy to sign an NDA if you want one.

Do you do Android?

Not yet. iOS only for now.

Tell me where you're stuck.

I reply by email, usually within a day. If you were rejected, paste the message from Apple and I'll tell you what it means for free.
